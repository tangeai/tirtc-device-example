# Sample Media Attribution

`sample_demo_mjpeg_pcma.avi` is an adapted excerpt from the Voice of America
Learning English program "News Words: Dire":

- Source page: https://learningenglish.voanews.com/a/4541102.html
- Program: VOA Learning English, News Words
- Published: October 4, 2018
- Source file: official 1080p direct download linked by the source page
- Rights policy: https://learningenglish.voanews.com/p/6861.html
- Rights note: VOA Learning English states that its texts, audio, photos and
  videos are public domain and may be reused with credit; third-party agency
  material is excluded from this excerpt

Changes made for this G32S10X example:

- selected 40.5-49.9 seconds, containing one presenter speaking continuously to
  camera with matching subtitles
- resized video to 1280x720 with Lanczos scaling
- converted video to MJPEG at 15 frames per second
- normalized speech to -18 LUFS integrated / -3 dBTP, then converted it to
  G.711 A-law (PCMA), 8 kHz, mono
- stored both streams from timestamp zero in a 9.4-second AVI container

The generated sample is 5,123,440 bytes and is used only as prerecorded test
media. No camera, microphone, speaker, JPEG accelerator, or audio peripheral is
initialized by this example.
